# The Isos fleet: lore

The Isos do not call it a navy. They call it the Sky Flock, and to them it is one more part of the Fold, doing its duty in the place the Shepherds gave it. Its officers are the Sky Caste. Its captains are Helms: a Second Helm commands a squadron, a First Helm a fleet, and over a whole region stands a High Helm, whose word on the void is the Shepherds' own.

Isos ships are built for patience. They are slow, careful and very well armed, and they fight at the furthest range their guns allow, from behind a screen of guided missiles and Vertex fighters. An Isos captain sees no glory in a close fight. Glory is for the lost. The Fold simply wins.

The Lattice is the Flock's great carrier, a ship like a cathedral, ringed with docks where Tethers hang like lanterns. A Tether is a small escort that never leaves its mother ship's side: it is clamped to her in the voyage and cast loose in the fight, and its crew are proud to be Tethered in the old sense too, bound to the Fold for life. Facet cruisers hold the line. Delta gunships carry the Guardians down to worlds that have not yet knelt.

Each region of the Fold raises its own fleet, Outring and Spire, Prism and Ember, Waygate, Hearth and Nexus. When a new system is to be Gathered, they all come.
