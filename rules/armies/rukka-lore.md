# The Rukka fleet: lore

Nobody builds a Rukka ship. It gets put together, usually on a match day, out of whatever the lads could carry off. A cruisa is three wrecks welded nose to tail with an engine strapped on the back and bangers sticking out of every hole. It flies, mostly. When it doesn't, the Spanners hit it.

The fleet goes where the Big Ruck goes. When a Gaffer has enough lads behind him and enough ships under them, he picks a fight with the biggest crowd in reach and the whole lot sets off together, singing. Each Firm sails under its own colours. The Boy Racers bolt on more engines than any sane ship could survive. The Wide Boyz buy their guns, or say they did. The Rag-and-Bone Lot fly ships nobody else would board. The Terrace Lot want to get stuck in; the Old School still swear by grabbers and a good hard ram.

Their gunnery is terrible and they know it, so they make up for it with more guns. Chuckas throw torpedoes in rough handfuls; buzzas and droppers swarm off the decks with no plan beyond the nearest target. When nothing else will do, a Lump comes along: a hollowed asteroid full of engines and lads, too big to stop and too daft to care.

To a Rukka captain the best part of any battle is the moment the hulls touch and the crews go over the rail, and the second best is telling the story afterwards.
