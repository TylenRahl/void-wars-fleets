# The Katharos fleet: lore

A Katharos fleet is not many ships. It is one mind with many bodies, and every hull in it is a hand the Katharos has reached out across the dark. There is no crew aboard, no captain and no bridge: only Drones walking the corridors, and Scrubbers crawling over the hull to mend whatever breaks.

The Katharos call their wars the Cleansing. They do not come to conquer a world or rule it. They come to clear it of flesh, as a surgeon clears a wound, and then they go away again.

Their ships look like dark crescents and spines, unlit, cold to every instrument. A Barrow, the greatest of them, moves without any drive another people would recognise: its null-mass drive lets it stop dead and turn about in a moment. Its storm arcs leap from hull to hull, and its flayers peel armour off in sheets.

Some Barrows carry an Ossuary, a vault at the ship's heart where the Katharos keeps a few captured minds of every race it has fought. It uses them as a mirror, studying how its enemies think, and when it turns the Ossuary's gaze on an enemy captain, that captain's thoughts go quiet and stay quiet.

Cryptships, Sickles and the small Vigil and Knell escorts make up the rest. They fight without hurry or fear. If a battle goes badly the whole fleet simply fades from the table, to come back somewhere else, years later, as patient as before.
