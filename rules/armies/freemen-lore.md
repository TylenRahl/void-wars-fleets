# The Freemen Navy: lore

A Freemen warship has no mind of its own, and its crew would scuttle it before they let it grow one. Nothing aboard thinks. The guns are laid by hand from brass sights, the reactors are tended by stokers with gauges and wrenches, and every course is plotted with pencil and dividers on paper charts that fill a whole deck.

The hardest part is the aether. Other peoples let a machine find the way between stars. The Freemen use Pathfinders: men and women who sit in a dark room at the prow and steer by feel, guessing at currents no instrument can show them. A good Pathfinder is worth more than a cruiser, and the fleet treats them that way.

So the Freemen build big and build plain. A Sovereign class battleship is a slab of armour with an engine at one end and gun decks down both flanks, crewed by twenty thousand souls who will never hear a machine whisper on their radios. Lantern cruisers hold the line, Cannonades lob sunburst shells at extreme range, and Merlin interceptors and Longwing bombers fly from hangars where pilots chalk their kills on the bulkheads.

The Navy guards the Rimward Reach from the Dominion, and the Dominion hates it for one reason above all: there is nothing aboard a Freemen ship for a Warden to talk to.

Its High Admirals have a rule for new captains. "If it can think, throw it out the airlock. If it can't, polish it."
