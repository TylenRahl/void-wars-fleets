# The Thnitoi fleet: lore

When the Hollow Court decides to feed, its ships cut loose from the great chained city and go hunting on their own. They are Void Pirates, every one, and they do not fight for ground, for honour or for plunder. They fight for crews.

A Thnitoi raider never comes as itself. Its false-flag engines sing with another ship's voice, so that a convoy sees a friend, or a merchant, or nothing at all, until the void fields drop and the hollow lances open fire. By then the Agony cruisers are already alongside.

The weapon the Court loves best is the Harpoon. A Thnitoi ship fires it into an enemy hull like a whaler's iron, and up the line come the takers, with nets and stunning blades, to carry off every living soul they can drag back. Lamprey torpedoes bite into an enemy's drive and hold it, so that a fast ship becomes a slow one and a slow one becomes a catch.

A raid is led by a Dread Void Prince, who keeps his place only as long as the catch keeps coming. Each Prince counts his captives the way other captains count kills, and the Court ranks its Princes by the size of their haul.

Their cousins of the Athanatoi Houses hunt for the risk. The Thnitoi have never seen the point of risk. A Kite frigate that comes home with three hundred captives and no scratch on its paint is, to them, a perfect voyage.
