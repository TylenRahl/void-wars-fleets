# The Phage fleet: lore

The Phage does not travel, but its children do. When a Thread has dragged a world close enough, the Mound sends out a Mound Fleet to meet it: living ships hatched in the Mound's deepest chambers, grown from the same chitin as its walls.

At the heart of every Mound Fleet swims a Queen ship. It is the Queen's voice in the void, a vast creature that breathes her scent into the space around it, and every ship in the fleet follows that scent the way an ant follows a trail. Close to a Queen ship, the swarm fights with terrible purpose. Far from one, a ship loses the Scent, and falls back on simpler wants: feed, flee, fight whatever is nearest.

The ships are weapons from end to end. Their claws close on enemy hulls and do not let go. They spit searbile, a living plasma that burns through armour, and burning acid that eats a hull from inside. Their flanks sprout brood cysts that pour out spores, a cloud that blinds and chokes ordnance before it arrives. Great Spore Mines drift ahead of the fleet like seeds on the wind.

The ships of a Mound Fleet are not built to come home. They are built to strip a world's defences in the months before it arrives at the Mound, and then to feed the Hunger themselves. Sailors in the Rimward Reach tell of the Gulper, the biggest of them, but nobody has seen one twice.
