# The Concord fleet: lore

A Concord ship is a Dyad. Its captain and its hull mind pledged to each other years before the first plate of armour was laid, and the ship was grown around the two of them. No admiral may give a captain a ship, and no shipyard may give a ship a captain. The pair chooses, or the hull is never launched.

That is why the Concord fleet is small, and why it is so hard to kill. When the bridge is smashed, the hull mind flies on alone. When the hull mind is overloaded, the captain steers by hand and instinct. Crews say a Concord ship has two hearts and needs only one of them.

Each Concord Host keeps a few great host barges, the mothers of its fleet. They carry the Host's ground war: Dropwings to put Dyads on a world's surface, and Deepflesh boarding parties, heavy Dyads in living armour made for one task, cutting through an enemy hull from the inside. Lance cruisers screen the barges, and Flare, Dirk, Tracker and Cutlass escorts run ahead.

The fleet answers to a Fleet Prime, chosen by the captains themselves. A Prime who has to give an order twice is not Prime for long.

The Concord fights the Dominion with a cold hatred it shows to no one else. Every Dominion ship has a Vessel sealed inside it, and every Concord crew knows how close they came to being one.
