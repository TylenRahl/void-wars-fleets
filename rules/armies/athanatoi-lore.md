# The Athanatoi fleet: lore

An Athanatoi ship sails on light. Its masts carry sails woven so fine that a sun's wind fills them, and it moves through a battle the way a hawk moves through weather: never straight, never still, always with the light at its back. Its mirage fields throw its shape across a thousand kilometres, so that enemy gunners fire at ghosts.

Every great House keeps a fleet, and every fleet is a Hunt waiting to happen. The Huntseer reads the battle before it starts and names the quarry. The Polemarch leads the ships against it. A House sails for one reason only: the chase, the only cure for the Stillness that the Athanatoi trust.

Some ships carry the Stillness with them. A Stillship is steered by its own dead: Athanatoi who fell into the crystal sleep long ago and were set into the hull, their unmoving minds still dimly turning, still faintly guiding the helm. Their kin say the stilled feel the hunt through the ship's bones, and that it is the closest thing to waking they will ever know.

Wyrmships lead the line with their starlances, Eos and Helios light cruisers launch Duskwing fighters and Aetos bombers, and the swift Asphodel and Foxglove frigates harry the edges. House Penthos, which has lost more of its people to the Stillness than any other, sails with almost nothing but Stillships, and the other Houses give its silent fleets a wide berth.
