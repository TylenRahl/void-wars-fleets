# 7. Damage, Critical Hits and Hulks

The guns of a capital ship can turn a city to glass, and ships are built to take it: thick armour,
shields and a hull braced to survive. What usually fails first is the crew, long before the hull
cracks or the drive bursts.

## Taking damage

- Every hit that is not stopped (by shields, a Brace for Impact! save or anything else) is **1 point
  of damage**. Note the damage on your roster.
- **Crippled.** A ship that has lost **half its starting hits** is crippled. A Lantern class cruiser
  has 8 hits, so it is crippled once it has taken 4 damage.
- **Out of action.** A ship that has lost all its hits rolls on the catastrophic damage table (below)
  to see whether it blows apart or drifts as a wreck.

### Crippled ships

A crippled ship:

- has its **shields, turrets, ordnance and all its weapons halved**,
- moves **5 cm slower**,
- may not fire a **sunburst cannon**, and
- is easier to board: the enemy adds +2 in a boarding action (chapter 8).

Halving always rounds up. Crippling and Brace for Impact! add together: a ship that is both has its
weapons and ordnance halved, then halved again, rounding up each time.

## Critical hits

The weapons of space are so destructive that any hit may wreck something vital. **For every point of
damage a ship takes, whatever caused it, roll a D6.** Hits stopped by shields don't count, as they did
no damage.

- On a 1-5, nothing more happens.
- On a **6**, the hit is a **critical hit**: roll 2D6 on the table below.
- Damage done by a critical hit never causes a critical hit of its own.
- **Escorts:** an escort that suffers a critical hit, for any reason (a boarding action included), is
  destroyed outright.

### The critical hits table

| 2D6 | Result | Extra damage |
|---|---|---|
| 2 | **Dorsal Armament Damaged.** The weapons along the top of the hull may not fire until repaired. | +0 |
| 3 | **Starboard Armament Damaged.** The weapons on the right side may not fire until repaired. | +0 |
| 4 | **Port Armament Damaged.** The weapons on the left side may not fire until repaired. | +0 |
| 5 | **Prow Armament Damaged.** The prow weapons may not fire until repaired. | +0 |
| 6 | **Engine Room Damaged.** The crew is fighting to save the reactors: the ship may make no turns until repaired. | +1 |
| 7 | **Fire!** Fires spread through the decks. Try to put it out in the End Phase (chapter 9). Each fire not put out does 1 more damage, and goes on burning. | +0 |
| 8 | **Thrusters Damaged.** The ship's speed is 10 cm lower until repaired. | +1 |
| 9 | **Bridge Smashed.** The command deck is wrecked: the ship's Resolve drops by 3. Cannot be repaired. If this is the flagship of the fleet commander, the fleet loses every commander's re-roll it has left for the rest of the battle (chapter 11). | +0 |
| 10 | **Shields Collapse.** The shield generators burn out: the ship's shields fall to 0. Cannot be repaired. | +0 |
| 11 | **Hull Breach.** A great tear opens in the hull. | +D3 |
| 12 | **Bulkhead Collapse.** Whole sections of the ship buckle and give way. | +D6 |

The extra damage of a critical is added to the ship's damage at once.

### The same critical twice

- Criticals that can be repaired add up. Each one must be repaired before the system works again: a
  ship with two Port Armament Damaged needs two repairs before its port guns fire.
- **Thrusters Damaged** slows the ship by 10 cm only once, however many it has, but every one of them
  must still be repaired before the ship has its full speed back.
- **Fires** add up. A ship with three fires that puts none of them out takes 3 damage, and all three go
  on burning.
- If a critical can't be applied, because the ship has no such weapons or it has already suffered that
  critical and it can't be repaired, apply the **next result up** the table instead. A ship with no
  dorsal weapons that rolls Dorsal Armament Damaged takes Starboard Armament Damaged.

## Catastrophic damage

A ship with no hits left can no longer fight. Some of its crew may be alive in sealed sections; some
of its power may still run. But it is out of the battle.

### Escorts

An escort reduced to 0 hits is removed. Put one Blast Marker as close as possible to where it was: the
cloud of its wreckage. A Blast Marker is smaller than a small base, so this marker can only take down a
nearby ship's shields if their bases overlapped.

### Capital ships

A capital ship reduced to 0 hits rolls 2D6 on the catastrophic damage table, to see whether its drive
overloads or its aether drive tears open.

| 2D6 | Result | Blast Markers |
|---|---|---|
| 2-6 | **Drifting Hulk.** The ship is a shattered wreck. In each of its later Manoeuvre Phases it moves 4D6 cm straight ahead. Put a Blast Marker touching its base after each move. | 1 |
| 7-8 | **Blazing Hulk.** A burning wreck, every deck on fire; in time the fires go out, or set it off. It moves 4D6 cm straight ahead in each of its later Manoeuvre Phases. After each move, put a Blast Marker touching its base and roll on this table again. | 1 |
| 9-11 | **Plasma Drive Overload.** The drive bursts in white-hot plasma. Remove the ship. Every ship within 3D6 cm of the wreck is struck by lance shots of Strength equal to **half** the ship's starting hits. | half its starting hits |
| 12 | **Aether Drive Implosion.** The aether drive tears a hole in space that savages everything near it. Remove the ship. Every ship within 3D6 cm is struck by lance shots of Strength equal to the ship's starting hits. | its starting hits |

- The lance shots of an explosion roll to hit as normal lances (4+).
- An explosion strikes all round (chapter 5): nothing blocks it, and being inside, outside or behind an
  asteroid field makes no difference to whether a ship is within its 3D6 cm.
- Shields work against an explosion as normal.
- **Placing an explosion's Blast Markers:** put one exactly where the ship was, then fan the rest around
  it, touching it, until all are placed. They don't overlap one another.
- A ship reduced to 0 hits in a **boarding action** becomes a drifting hulk at once, without a roll
  (chapter 8). If a critical hit is what took its last hit, roll on the table as normal.

## Hulks

A drifting or blazing hulk stays on the table.

- Hulks **block lines of fire** across their base: the gas and debris pouring off them hide what lies
  behind.
- A hulk has no shields and no other protection of that kind. Its turrets count as 0, so bombers take
  nothing off their attacks.
- **Enemy ships may fire on a hulk.** They don't need a Resolve Test to ignore it when it is the nearest
  target. To fire at an enemy hulk that is not the nearest target, they need the test as usual.
- When shooting at a hulk, use the destroyed ship's Armour to roll for hits.
- Each time a ship, squadron or ordnance wave inflicts hits on a hulk by shooting or with ordnance, roll
  **once** on the catastrophic damage table, however many hits it scored. In the game, that roll leaves no
  second Blast Marker; the hulk only leaves the one after each drift.
- A player may not fire on, or board, **his own** hulks, to deny the enemy victory points or to set them
  off. Torpedoes that happen to strike a friendly hulk hit it normally, but may not be aimed at one.
- A hulk that drifts into an asteroid field or a planet is destroyed. One that drifts into an aether rift
  vanishes (chapter 10).
