# VOID WARS: Fleets rules book

The rules of the game in plain Markdown, chapter by chapter, plus a short piece of lore for each fleet.
Every distance is in centimetres. The game's code in `src/rules/` plays these rules; where the two ever
disagree, report it as a bug.

## Core rules (`core/`)
1. `01-the-game-and-the-dice.md`: what the game is, what you need, and how the dice are rolled.
2. `02-ships-and-their-profiles.md`: the ship profile, the kinds of ship, bases, and Resolve.
3. `03-the-turn.md`: who goes first and the order of the phases.
4. `04-manoeuvre-and-special-orders.md`: moving and turning, and the six special orders.
5. `05-fire.md`: weapons batteries, lances, the gunnery table, shields, Blast Markers and the sunburst cannon.
6. `06-ordnance.md`: torpedoes, attack craft and turrets.
7. `07-damage-and-critical-hits.md`: damage, critical hits and hulks.
8. `08-boarding-ramming-and-hit-and-run.md`: ramming, boarding actions and Hit-and-Run raids.
9. `09-the-end-phase.md`: the End Phase, repairs and disengaging.
10. `10-celestial-phenomena.md`: gas clouds, asteroid fields, planets, low orbit and the other hazards.
11. `11-fleets-squadrons-and-commanders.md`: choosing a fleet, squadrons, commanders, setting up and victory points.
12. `12-tides-of-the-archipelago.md`: Tides of the Archipelago, the same battles fought by sailing ships at sea.
