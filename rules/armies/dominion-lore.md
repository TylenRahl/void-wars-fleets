# The Dominion fleet: lore

Every Dominion warship is a Harrow on a vast scale. Its Warden is the ship: the drive, the guns, the hull's ten thousand sensors. Sealed in a cradle at its heart lies the captain, a Vessel claimed young and wired into the bridge, so that the ship can borrow the speed of a human mind when the battle turns strange.

The Contest is fought in the hulls too. A Warden ship flies perfectly and predictably. A ship whose Vessel has broken its Warden flies like a brawler, and the Dominion sends such ships where the fighting is worst. At the top stands a Harrow Admiral, who rules a fleet's Wardens and who may himself be a machine or a Usurper; only the Dominion knows which.

Before a battle the Wardens load their Protocols, the four old doctrines of war written into the Dominion's code. Wrath makes a ship hungry to close and board. Endurance makes it stubborn, sealing its compartments against intruders. Rapture fills the aether around it with noise that dulls enemy minds. Cipher gives its Warden one more answer to every problem. A Harrow Lord may carry one Protocol; a Harrow Admiral may carry four.

Their Sunderer battleships launch clouds of Carrion fighters, Bloodshed and Malice cruisers fight broadside to broadside, and Gravehook pods carry the Kept's champions across. Sometimes the fleet drags a derelict with it: a mountain of welded wrecks, every one of them a Warden that once lost.
