# 3. The Turn

## Who goes first

The battle you are playing says who takes the first turn. Usually both players roll a D6 and the
higher scorer decides whether to go first or second. Some battles settle it for you: in an ambush,
the side springing the trap always moves first.

## How long a battle lasts

A battle normally goes on until one side gives up or has no ships left on the table. A battle may
instead last a set number of game turns, or end at once when one side achieves a sudden victory,
such as destroying the enemy flagship. The battle you are playing says which.

## Taking turns

The players take turns. In your turn you move and fight with all your ships; then your opponent
does the same, and so on. To keep things clear, a turn is split into phases: you move every ship
you want to move, then fire with every ship that can, and so on.

Some things happen out of turn. A ship may Brace for Impact! in the enemy's turn (chapter 4), and
both players move ordnance in every Ordnance Phase. When something like this interrupts a turn,
deal with it and then carry on with the turn where it left off.

## The turn sequence

Your turn has four phases, always in this order:

1. **Manoeuvre Phase.**
   - First remove the special order markers you placed last turn. A ship on Brace for Impact!
     keeps its marker: that order lasts until the end of the ship's next turn.
   - Then move your ships and squadrons one at a time. Before you move a ship or squadron you may
     take a Command check to put it on special orders.
   - Once one Command check fails, you may take no more this turn (Brace for Impact! aside).
   - A ship must move at least half its speed unless it is on Burn Retros.

   See chapter 4.
2. **Fire Phase.** Your ships fire their weapons batteries, lances and sunburst cannon at enemy
   ships in range. Torpedoes and attack craft are launched at the end of the phase. See chapter 5.
3. **Ordnance Phase.** **Both** players move all their torpedoes and attack craft already on the
   table. You, the player whose turn it is, move all of yours first. See chapter 6.
4. **End Phase.** In this order:
   1. Boarding actions and Hit-and-Run attacks are fought (chapter 8).
   2. Both players try to repair critical damage on their ships (chapter 9).
   3. You, the player whose turn it is, remove D6 Blast Markers from the table (chapter 9).

The End Phase is also the moment to tidy the table, check the battle's victory conditions and
count victory points.

When your End Phase is over, your turn ends and your opponent's begins.

## The start of a game turn

A few things are rolled for at the start of each **game turn** (before the first player's turn),
not at the start of each player's turn: solar flares and radiation bursts (chapter 10) are the
ones in this book.
